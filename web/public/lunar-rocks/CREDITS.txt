The wrist display reports the approximate physical dimensions of each curated
piece as scanned. The coordinate-registered OBJ files above and their Model
Parameters PDFs specify metres. We projected the original mesh vertices onto
their three principal axes, took the span along each axis, and converted to
centimetres before resizing the meshes for the simulation. Values are rounded
to the nearest millimetre.

The VR viewer uses reduced meshes and photographic textures from the three returned specimens. Instances are oriented and placed by the simulation. Their regional size and spacing estimates use the survey data below.
