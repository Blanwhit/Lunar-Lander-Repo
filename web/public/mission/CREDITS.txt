Moon viewer adaptations include real-world scaling, lower mesh density, consolidated materials, thermal blanket surface detail and window materials.

Lunar Module exterior geometry adapted from NASA / Michael D. Carbajal. The fitted thermal shields, equipment pallet, hatch, quad housings, strut insulation and radar hardware are visual reconstructions from Apollo 11 photographs and press-kit diagrams.

Eagle landing sequence
Three surface-sensing probes fold independently against the simulated terrain. The Apollo 11 press kit supplies the nominal one-second crew response used in the simulation. The Apollo Lunar Surface Journal documents the actual contact call and shutdown.
