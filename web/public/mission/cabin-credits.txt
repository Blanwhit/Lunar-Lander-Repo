Apollo cockpit interiors

The lunar module atlas uses instrument panels 1–4, the commander's breakers,
the docking hatch, and aft stowage from NASA LM-11 closeout photographs
LM11-co46, LM11-co13, LM11-co28, and LM11-co2. Scans by Paul Fjeld from
NASA negatives supplied by the Northrop Grumman History Center. The
photographs show Orion, the Apollo 16 lunar module.
https://apollojournals.org/alsj/a16/a16LMCloseOutPhotos.html
https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/

The commander's forward-window frame uses NASA photograph 69-H-134, scan
by Frederic Artner. The photographed window opening was cut away to reveal
the live simulation.
https://apollojournals.org/alsj/a11/ap11-69-H-134.jpg

Command module instrument imagery uses Apollo 11 Columbia, NASM-SI-98-16043,
by Eric F. Long, Smithsonian National Air and Space Museum. Smithsonian
Open Access, CC0 1.0. The images were cropped and rectified for the atlas.
https://ids.si.edu/ids/deliveryService?id=NASM-SI-98-16043
https://www.si.edu/object/nasm_A19700102000
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The interactive cabin shells, window recesses, landing sight and illuminated
readouts are reconstructions for the simulation.
