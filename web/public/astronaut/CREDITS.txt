eva-suit.glb
Extravehicular Mobility Unit by NASA / Michael D. Carbajal
Model and source: https://science.nasa.gov/3d-resources/extravehicular-mobility-unit/
NASA 3D Resources: https://science.nasa.gov/3d-resources/

Lunar Walk adapted the EMU body to its first-person skeleton, removed the
helmet and maneuvering unit, and added lunar overboots, tread, gaiters,
pockets, cloth folds and woven material. CraterView replaced the model's gloves
with procedural pressurized EVA gloves (web/src/suitGlove.ts) and added knee
and elbow convolutes, leg seams and lunar dust in the suit's shader. The avatar combines an orbital EVA suit
reference with purpose-built surface footwear.
