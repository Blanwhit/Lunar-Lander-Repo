Scan method and location: https://blog.polyhaven.com/moon/

The team photographed a lunar regolith simulant on Earth at Spaceport Rostock, Germany. The VR viewer repeats this 4 × 4 metre material as representative local surface detail. The original material's displacement scale is 0.010 m with a midlevel of 0.5.
