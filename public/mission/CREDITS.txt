Moon viewer adaptations include real-world scaling, lower mesh density, consolidated materials, thermal blanket surface detail, window materials, and separable Saturn V stages. The command and service module represents the Apollo spacecraft family.

Lunar Module exterior geometry adapted from NASA / Michael D. Carbajal. The fitted thermal shields, equipment pallet, hatch, quad housings, strut insulation and radar hardware are visual reconstructions from Apollo 11 photographs and press-kit diagrams.
NASA/JSC photographs AS11-40-5927 and AS11-44-6581

Saturn V propulsion and launch escape hardware, the CSM docking collar, crew windows, radiators and deployed four-reflector S-band antenna were reconstructed from NASA references. Stage and service-module interiors are simplified for real-time display. The departure sequence compresses first-stage and interstage separation into one event.
